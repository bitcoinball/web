Why not just buy a lottery ticket?

A traditional lottery is a negative-sum game by design. Bitcoin mining pays you from newly issued value.

Traditional lottery BitcoinBall machine
What you pay to play Every ticket, every week — recurring spend, gone for good One-time machine purchase + electricity. Nothing else, ever
Payout rate (return to players) 50–65% Paid from block subsidy — new value, not other players’ losses
Draw frequency 1–2 per week ~144 per day (every ~10 minutes)
What’s left when you don’t win Nothing — ticket expires worthless A working appliance + hardware resale value
Long-run expectation Guaranteed loss for players as a group Transparent, computable — varies with price & difficulty

Mining, explained simply

Your machine tries trillions of numbers per second and reports to the pre-installed third-party pool (several established pools ship with the device). When the pool finds a block, it shares the reward by hashrate — that’s FPPS.
